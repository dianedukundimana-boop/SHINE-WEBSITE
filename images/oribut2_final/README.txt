ORIBUT / SHINE MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE

Open index.html to start. Pages: About, Products, Hair Care, Personal Care, Home & Laundry Care, Why Oribut, Careers, Contact, Order.

For XAMPP: copy the folder into htdocs and open http://localhost/oribut2_final/

The order page sends order requests to WhatsApp. Contact form opens the visitor email client. No backend/database is included in this rebuild.
